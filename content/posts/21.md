**NARRATOR** – The door closes behind you. You descend the creaking stairs, pass the wary landlady, and finally step outside. A cool wind immediately hits your face, carrying away the last traces of warmth, sandalwood, and orange oil. For several moments, you stand beneath the awning in silence, looking out over the half-empty square.

**ARTISTRY** [Challenging: Success] – It smells of damp earth, wet stone, and something else — something impossible to name, barely perceptible, as though somewhere nearby a stove is burning, clothes are drying, a kettle is being put on, and someone is waiting for somebody to finally come home.

**DISCERNMENT** [Medium: Success] – He knows the name. And clearly, it's not one people are supposed to say out loud at the entrance.

**GRAVE'S THUG** – Damn it... Fine. Wait here and behave like good boys.

**GRAVE** – Well?.. So you found out about Elżbieta. Congratulations. And what of it?

**NARRATOR** – Roche says nothing. Without waiting for an invitation, he pulls back the bench beside Rat and sits down at the table as calmly as though he'd come not to a thief-in-law's den, but to a late supper with an old acquaintance. Then, with a casual motion, he picks up the jug and fills the empty glass.

**DOMINANCE** [Medium: Success] – A powerful statement: you didn't come here to beg for an audience, and you have no intention of standing before Grave like servants who've done something wrong.

**NARRATOR** – At first, you consider following your partner's example, but in the end you stop awkwardly a couple of steps from the table.

**VERNON ROCHE** – First of all, let's return to our conversation yesterday. Specifically, to the point where we established that the hanging was merely an attempt to conceal the true cause of death.

**GRAVE** – *We* didn't establish shit, Roche. You and that mutant are just spouting bullshit and trying to bluff me, for fuck's sake!

**NARRATOR** – Vernon smirks and helps himself to some sauerkraut. You watch him enviously. Apparently, the angrier Grave gets, the better your partner's appetite becomes.

**ROCK** – Listen, assholes, if you're planning to keep playing the same fucking tune as yesterday, you'd better get out now while I'm still in a good mood!

**GRAVE** – Ha. Maybe so. But first and foremost, I'm a businessman, and another corpse turning up in the neighborhood sure as hell won't make me any money. So... Nobody's touching your precious whore. I just said I don't want to see her. She can FUCK RIGHT OFF!!!

The people he curses at, threatens to knock their teeth out — and still thinks of as something close to family. Because he has no other family.

**NARRATOR** – He scratches his nose meaningfully.

**VERNON ROCHE** – And then she asked you for help? How did the idea of staging the hanging come about?

**GRAVE** – Well, Karmen was the one who suggested it. Said something like, why don't we fucking hang him and cover our tracks. Begged us to make damn sure her name never came up, otherwise... Well, sounds like she's already told you about her problems, so I won't go over all that again.
