In front of the massive oak door leading to the captain's office stands a lone fair-haired soldier with a halberd. He sways slightly, like a sailor who's just come ashore after a long voyage. **JETHRO** – Yes, pan!

**NARRATOR** – Jethro's right eye, still twitching rhythmically, remains fixed on Vernon. The left suddenly drifts off to the side and locks directly onto you.

**BOND OF STEEL** [Easy: Success] – Even in this state, he instinctively twists and wriggles his way out of trouble. It's simply in his nature. **VICE** – Oh, we'll come back to the powder later!

**FORTITUDE** – No. We will not be coming back to the powder. That matter is closed. Permanently.

**NARRATOR** – Vernon turns back to the soldier, who is now staring at the commander of the Blue Stripes like a cornered animal.

**DISCERNMENT** [Challenging: Success] – You hear Jethro's stomach begin to growl loudly.

**VERNON ROCHE** – Well, what are you gawking at me for? Move it, open the door. We have important business with the captain of the guard, and no time to stand around here dealing with you.

**NARRATOR** – Jethro nods hastily, but can't seem to work out which hand should grab the door handle and which should hold the halberd.

**VERNON ROCHE** – What are you fumbling with now?!

**NARRATOR** – The unfortunate guard somehow pins the halberd under one elbow, smacks himself in the forehead with the haft, and pulls the door open with both hands.

**VERNON ROCHE** – And get yourself into some kind of proper state! If I ever see you on duty like this again, I promise you'll regret it dearly. And just what Captain Meis is doing while all this goes on, I'd very much like to know!

Directly opposite the entrance stands a wooden desk, behind which sits a slightly heavyset man of about forty-five. His hair is cut short, though the dark strands still insist on curling like the fleece of a young ram. His face is flat, almost ordinary-looking. But his intelligent eyes watch you with calm attention. For some reason, that gaze makes you uneasy, and your hand begins to drift behind your back of its own accord.

**UMBRA** [Challenging: Failure] – Can you feel it?.. Something is wrong here.

**DIALECTIC** [Easy: Success] – A remarkably informative observation.

**YOU** – No. What am I supposed to feel?

**YOU** – What prey?

**REFLEX** – Unknown.

**NARRATOR** – The medallion is still twitching insistently.

**REFLEX** – Just stay alert.

**NARRATOR** – Meanwhile, the captain of the guard rises leisurely from behind his desk. You can't help noticing that he's rather short; beside him, Vernon looks like a giant.

**BRAWN** [Medium: Success] – But he's broad-shouldered, stocky, and built like a dwarf. Heavy fists, too. Don't underestimate him.

**VINCENT MEIS** – Good day, panowie.

**NARRATOR** – His voice is low and fairly pleasant. Even so, you feel no warmth toward the man whatsoever. There is something about him — something hard to define — that you instinctively find repellent.

**VINCENT MEIS** – Well, I certainly never expected to see Vernon Roche himself in my humble office!

**NARRATOR** – He gives your companion a sly, almost friendly smile. Almost.

**VINCENT MEIS** – Quite a dressing-down you gave poor Jethro. My compliments! Rest assured, the captain — yours truly — keeps a very close eye on things.

**NARRATOR** – He takes his time walking around the desk and comes to a stop in front of you.

**VINCENT MEIS** – Private Jethro is, of course, a drunk, a drug addict, and, to put it plainly, a rare bastard.

**NARRATOR** – He says it in the tone of a man praising his favorite son. With tenderness and a trace of hidden pride.

**VINCENT MEIS** – But he knows his job. More importantly, he follows orders without hesitation and without asking questions.

**SENSIBILITY** [Medium: Success] – He speaks of his subordinate as though Jethro were a useful tool, suited to certain kinds of work. Delicate work, most likely.

**NARRATOR** – Your companion stands like a statue, still saying nothing. It's impossible to tell whether he's even listening to the portly captain's little speech.

**VINCENT MEIS** – A man like that always comes in handy, you know... But Jethro will, of course, be punished for his misconduct.

**NARRATOR** – The captain extends a broad hand to Vernon and smiles again.

**VINCENT MEIS** – Vincent Meis, captain of the Temple Quarter guard. Though, of course, you already know that.

**VERNON ROCHE** – Vernon Roche, commander of the Blue Stripes. Though, of course, you already know that as well.

**SENSIBILITY** [Challenging: Success] – They're like two dogs meeting in the street. Sniffing each other out, neither quite sure yet whether it's time to growl.

**VERNON ROCHE** – You understand correctly. We require whatever assistance you can reasonably provide with the investigation.
